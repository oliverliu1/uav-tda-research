Seed 123 hit 139 timeouts on C2 manifold W_2 calls (~4.6% of 3 dim x 1000 = 3000 candidate calls). Timed-out homology dimensions contributed zero to their flow's combined-W_2 sum, which depresses the c2 scores for the affected flows and may inflate variance on c2-containing AUC combinations. This is disclosed here rather than baked into the verdict heuristic.
