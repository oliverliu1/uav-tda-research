# legacy_2026-04 — quarantined April 2026 figures (do not use)

These 15 PNGs were committed on 2026-04-11/12 (commits `bb47971`, `94c3115`) and moved here
in Phase 8 (2026-11-09). **Never use them in the manuscript, slides, or any new document.**
Current manuscript figures are vector PDFs in `results/figures/manuscript/`, regenerated by
`uav-tda artifacts` from committed CSVs (see `paper/MANUSCRIPT_ARTIFACTS.md`).

## Why quarantined

- **Provenance:** produced by the pre-monolith numbered scripts (now in
  `archive/scripts_archive/`), not by `pipeline.py` or `uav_tda/`. No provenance sidecars; the
  numbers behind them cannot be traced to a committed CSV.
- **Leakage-era lineage:** they predate the current splits and share lineage with the
  analysis that `archive/README.md` documents as having a data-leakage flaw. Their
  supervised/"TDA improves accuracy" framing is dead (see `CLAUDE/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`).
- **Not printable:** `paper/historical/2026-05-drafting/FIGURE_AUDIT.md` rates every one of them
  TOO_SMALL — 7.7–17.9 in wide at 300 DPI, needing a 0.20–0.45× shrink for a 3.5 in column
  (estimated 2–4.5 pt print text).
